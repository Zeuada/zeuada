zeuada.com has a new site. It's deliberately small: a page for Unloop, the research behind it, support and privacy information, and this updates page.

The site practises what Unloop is built around. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics or third-party scripts, and has no pop-ups or newsletter prompts. It's a static site, so pages load fast even on a slow connection.

New releases, fixes and research notes will be posted here, with dates.
