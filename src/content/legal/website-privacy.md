This notice covers the website at zeuada.com. The Unloop app has its own [privacy policy](/unloop/privacy/).

## What this site collects

Nearly nothing.

- **No cookies.** The site doesn't set any cookies, so there's no cookie banner.
- **No analytics or trackers.** There are no analytics tools, advertising pixels, social media embeds or third-party scripts.
- **No third-party fonts or assets.** Fonts and images are served from zeuada.com itself, so visiting doesn't tell anyone else you were here.

## Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. We don't use this information to identify or track visitors. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for how it handles this data.

## Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

## Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, such as Google Play or the research papers we cite. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR. Email [privacy@zeuada.com](mailto:privacy@zeuada.com) with any question or request.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change and we'll note it on the [updates page](/updates/).
