This policy explains what the Unloop Android app ("Unloop") does with information on your phone. Unloop is made by Zeuada (<span class="placeholder">[CONFIRM: registered legal name and entity type]</span>), referred to here as "we".

## The short version

- Unloop uses Android's accessibility service only to know **which Instagram screen is open** and **when you swipe**.
- It **does not read** your messages, captions, comments, or anything outside Instagram.
- It **does not record** which reels you watch or what they contain.
- Your counts, feelings and check-ins are **stored on your phone**.
- Crash reports contain no personal data or content details.
- No ads. We don't sell data.

## How Unloop uses the accessibility service

Unloop needs Android's accessibility service to recognise when the Instagram Reels screen is open, so it can show the pause and keep the reel counter up to date. Android doesn't offer another way for an app to know this.

Unloop uses the accessibility service only to:

- detect whether the screen currently open in Instagram is the Reels feed, and
- detect when you swipe to the next reel, so it can count reels and time.

Unloop does not use the accessibility service to read, collect, store or share the content of your screen, your messages, what you type, or anything in other apps. You can turn the accessibility service off at any time in your phone's settings; Unloop will stop pausing Reels until you turn it back on.

## Information stored on your phone

Unloop keeps the following on your device so it can work:

- daily counts of reels and minutes on the Reels feed,
- the feelings you select at a pause and your answers to check-ins,
- which activities you chose and how you rated them,
- your settings, such as your daily budget, Reels-free windows and the identity statement you write.

This information stays on your phone. We don't have access to it, and it isn't sent to us.

## Crash reports

If Unloop crashes, it may send a crash report so we can fix the problem. Crash reports contain technical details such as the app version, device model, Android version and the error that occurred. They contain no personal data and nothing about your Instagram use or the content you view.

Crash reports are processed by <span class="placeholder">[CONFIRM: crash-reporting provider, e.g. Firebase Crashlytics, and a link to its privacy policy]</span>.

## What we don't do

- We don't show ads.
- We don't sell, rent or trade data.
- We don't use your data to build advertising profiles.
- We don't share your data with anyone, except the crash-report processing described above.

## Deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your phone, you control it. Clearing Unloop's storage (Settings → Apps → Unloop → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstalling the app deletes it.
